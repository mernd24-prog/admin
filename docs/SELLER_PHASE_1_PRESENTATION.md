# Seller Panel Phase 1 Presentation

Audience: Seller onboarding and product management walkthrough
Status: Current seller-facing scope

## 1. Presentation Goal

This presentation introduces the seller to the currently available seller panel flow:

- Seller onboarding and KYC verification
- Basic dashboard access after verification
- Product Management with two active areas: Product Listing and Add Product

The goal is to keep seller expectations clear. The current phase is focused on getting sellers verified and helping them start creating their catalog.

## 2. Current Scope Summary

| Area | Available Now | Seller Message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onboarding | Yes | Complete seller profile, KYC details, documents, and required bank details. |
| KYC Status | Yes | After submission, the account moves to review. Sellers can see pending, approved, or update-required screens. |
| Dashboard | Basic/static view | The dashboard is available as a starter overview screen. Live business insights will expand later. |
| Product Listing | Yes | Sellers can view their product catalog entries from Product Management. |
| Add Product | Yes | Sellers can create a new product listing using the product form. |
| More modules | Coming soon | Locations, category tree, inventory, order flows, and other related seller tools will be added in upcoming phases. |

## 3. Slide Deck

### Slide 1: Welcome To The Seller Panel

The seller panel is being launched in phases.

In this first phase, the focus is simple:

- Complete onboarding
- Submit KYC
- Access the dashboard
- Start adding products
- View product listings

### Slide 2: What Sellers Can Do Today

Current active areas:

- Onboarding and KYC verification
- Dashboard
- Product Management
- Product Listing
- Add Product

Other modules are planned for upcoming releases.

### Slide 3: Seller Onboarding Flow

Seller onboarding helps verify the business before full panel access.

Main steps:

1. Register or log in to the seller panel.
2. Complete business/profile information.
3. Enter KYC details.
4. Upload required KYC documents.
5. Add required bank details.
6. Submit for verification.
7. Track the verification status from the seller status screen.

### Slide 4: KYC Information Collected

The KYC form captures the seller identity and business verification details needed to activate the account.

Typical information includes:

- Legal name
- Contact details
- PAN details
- Aadhaar details, when required
- GST or business details, when available
- Required document uploads

### Slide 5: Verification Status

After onboarding is submitted, sellers see a status screen.

Possible seller-facing states:

- Under review
- Approved
- Update required

When the account is approved, the seller can continue to the dashboard.

### Slide 6: Dashboard

The dashboard is available as the seller landing page after verification.

For the current phase, it is a basic/static overview screen. It gives sellers a first look at the panel structure while live analytics and operational metrics are added in later phases.

### Slide 7: Product Management

Product Management currently focuses on two actions:

- Product Listing
- Add Product

This keeps the first release focused and easy for sellers to understand.

### Slide 8: Product Listing

The Product Listing page helps sellers view catalog entries in one place.

Sellers can use it to:

- See existing product entries
- Search or filter the list
- Open product details where available
- Continue managing catalog information from the product area

### Slide 9: Add Product

The Add Product flow lets sellers create a product listing.

The form supports core catalog information such as:

- Product name and basic details
- Category selection, where available
- Pricing and stock information
- Product images/media
- Product type and variant information, where applicable
- SEO and tags, where applicable

### Slide 10: What Is Coming Soon

The seller panel will continue to expand.

Planned upcoming areas include:

- Location setup
- Category tree support
- More catalog controls
- Inventory-related tools
- Order-related workflows
- Seller operations and reporting enhancements

For now, sellers should focus on completing onboarding and adding product listings.

## 4. Seller Walkthrough Script

Use this script during a demo or seller training call.

Welcome to the seller panel. In this first phase, we are enabling the core starting flow for sellers. You will complete onboarding, submit KYC details, wait for verification, and then access the dashboard.

Once your account is verified, you will see the dashboard. The dashboard is currently a starter overview screen. More live data and operational insights will be added in upcoming phases.

The main working area available now is Product Management. In this phase, Product Management has two important actions: Product Listing and Add Product. Product Listing lets you view your catalog entries, while Add Product lets you create new product listings.

More modules are coming soon, including location setup, category tree support, and additional seller management tools. For now, the priority is onboarding, verification, and product catalog creation.

## 6. Scope Boundaries For This Phase

Use this section to keep demos and seller communication focused.

Available now:

- Onboarding
- KYC submission and verification status

- Product Listing
- Add Product

## 7. Short Seller-Facing Summary

The seller panel is currently available for onboarding, KYC verification, dashboard access, and product catalog creation. Sellers can complete their verification, open the dashboard after approval, view product listings, and add new products. More modules such as locations, category tree, inventory, orders, and reporting will be added soon.
